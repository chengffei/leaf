What is Leaf?Leaf 是什么？

Leaf is a free, open-source ebook reader for Mac. Double-click an EPUB, MOBI or AZW3 file to read it; close the window and Leaf quits. No library, no copies, no account. It remembers where you stopped, and reads like a Kindle. Native SwiftUI app, about 2.7 MB, macOS 15 or later. Not affiliated with Amazon. Leaf 是免费开源的 Mac 电子书阅读器。双击 EPUB、MOBI 或 AZW3 文件即可阅读，关窗即退出。没有书库，不复制文件，不要账号。它记得你读到哪，读起来像 Kindle。原生 SwiftUI 应用，约 2.7 MB，需要 macOS 15。与 Amazon 无关。

常见问题FAQ

安装和使用中最常被问到的几件事。Common questions about installing and using Leaf.

How do I open MOBI and AZW3 files on a Mac?怎么在 Mac 上打开 MOBI 和 AZW3 文件？

Install Leaf, then double-click the file in Finder, or right-click it and choose Open With › Leaf. The file must be DRM-free.装好 Leaf 后，在访达里双击文件，或右键选「打开方式 › Leaf」。文件需要无 DRM。

Is there a Kindle-like ebook reader for Mac?Mac 上有像 Kindle 一样的电子书阅读器吗？

Leaf reads DRM-free EPUB, MOBI and AZW3 files the Kindle way: time left from your own reading speed, location numbers, typography presets, footnotes in place and find in book. Highlights and notes come next. No store, no account.Leaf 用 Kindle 的方式读 Mac 上无 DRM 的 EPUB、MOBI、AZW3：按你的速度估算剩余时间、位置号、排版预设、脚注就地弹出、全书查找。高亮与笔记即将推出。没有商店，不用账号。

How is Leaf different from Readest?Leaf 和 Readest 有什么区别？

Readest is cross-platform, with a library, sync, highlights and text-to-speech. Leaf is Mac only and does less on purpose: no library, only your reading position, and it quits when you close the book. About 2.7 MB, against 67 MB.Readest 跨平台，有书库、同步、高亮和朗读。Leaf 只做 Mac，有意做得更少：没有书库，只记阅读位置，合上书就退出。约 2.7 MB，Readest 约 67 MB。

Is Leaf free?Leaf 免费吗？

Yes. Leaf is free and open source under the MIT License.免费，并以 MIT 协议开源。

Why does macOS say it can’t verify the developer?为什么 macOS 提示无法验证开发者？

Leaf isn’t notarized by Apple. Open it once and dismiss the warning, then go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. You only need to do this once.Leaf 没有经过 Apple 公证。先打开一次并关掉提示，再到「系统设置 › 隐私与安全性」点「仍要打开」。只需操作一次。

Can Leaf open Kindle books I bought?能打开在 Kindle 商店买的书吗？

Only DRM-free files. Books bought from the Kindle Store usually have DRM, and Leaf can’t open them.只能打开无 DRM 的文件。Kindle 商店购买的书通常带 DRM，Leaf 打不开。

Is Leaf a Kindle app?Leaf 是 Kindle 的 app 吗？

No. Leaf is an independent open-source project, not affiliated with Amazon. It borrows the reading experience of a Kindle — typography, progress, find, and soon highlights and notes — for DRM-free EPUB, MOBI and AZW3 files on your Mac. There is no store and no account.不是。Leaf 是独立的开源项目，与 Amazon 无关。它借鉴的是 Kindle 的阅读体验：排版、进度、查找，接下来是高亮与笔记，用来读 Mac 上无 DRM 的 EPUB、MOBI、AZW3。没有商店，不用账号。

Does Leaf have a library or bookshelf?Leaf 有书架或书库吗？

No. Leaf opens books where they are, without importing or copying them. Leaf itself keeps only where you stopped in each book; like any Mac document app, macOS lists recent files under File › Open Recent.没有。Leaf 直接打开书所在的位置，不导入也不复制文件。Leaf 自己只记每本读到哪；和其他 Mac 文档应用一样，系统会在「文件 › 打开最近使用」里列出最近的文件。
