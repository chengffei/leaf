安装Install

Leaf 是个人开源项目，没有经过 Apple 公证。第一次打开需要手动放行一次，之后就和普通 App 一样。 Leaf is an independent open-source project and isn’t notarized by Apple. Allow it once on first launch; after that it opens like any other app.

下载 Leaf.dmgDownload Leaf.dmg

拖进 ApplicationsDrag to Applications

打开下载的 Leaf.dmg，把 Leaf 拖到 Applications 文件夹。Open Leaf.dmg and drag Leaf into the Applications folder.

先打开一次Open it once

双击 Leaf，系统提示无法验证开发者，点「完成」。Double-click Leaf. When macOS says it can’t verify the developer, click Done.

在设置里放行Allow in Settings

「系统设置 › 隐私与安全性」拉到底部，点「仍要打开」。In System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.

需要 macOS 15 或更高。从 Kindle 商店购买的带 DRM 电子书无法打开。 Requires macOS 15 or later. Books with DRM, such as Kindle Store purchases, can’t be opened.
