What is Leaf?

Leaf is a free, open-source ebook reader for macOS. It opens EPUB, MOBI and AZW3 files directly from Finder without importing them into a library, remembers where you stopped reading, and quits when you close the window. It is built with SwiftUI and WebKit, uses foliate-js for rendering, and requires macOS 15 or later.

Less, on purpose

Leaf does one thing: get you to the first line of your book as fast as possible.

Three formats

Open EPUB, MOBI and AZW3 directly — no conversion needed.

Picks up where you left off

Books are recognized by content, so renaming or moving a file keeps your place.

Native contents sidebar

Press ⌘T. The current chapter is highlighted; click to jump.

Type that suits you

Font size from 80% to 200%, three line-spacing presets, original, sans or serif fonts.

Close means quit

No background process, no library window. Close the book and it’s gone.

Small and native

SwiftUI and WebKit, about 2.4 MB, follows light and dark appearance.

Contents, one key away

The sidebar is the system’s own. The highlight follows as you read, and a click on the page tucks it away again.

Leaf vs Apple Books vs Calibre

Apple Books and Calibre are both good tools. Leaf is for when you only want to open a file and read it.

Install

Leaf is an independent open-source project and isn’t notarized by Apple. Allow it once on first launch; after that it opens like any other app.

Download Leaf.dmg

Drag to Applications

Open Leaf.dmg and drag Leaf into the Applications folder.

Open it once

Double-click Leaf. When macOS says it can’t verify the developer, click Done.

Allow in Settings

In System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.

Requires macOS 15 or later. Books with DRM, such as Kindle Store purchases, can’t be opened.

FAQ

Common questions about installing and using Leaf.

How do I open MOBI and AZW3 files on a Mac?

Install Leaf, then double-click the file in Finder, or right-click it and choose Open With › Leaf. The file must be DRM-free.

Is Leaf free?

Yes. Leaf is free and open source under the MIT License.

Why does macOS say it can’t verify the developer?

Leaf isn’t notarized by Apple. Open it once and dismiss the warning, then go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. You only need to do this once.

Can Leaf open Kindle books I bought?

Only DRM-free files. Books bought from the Kindle Store usually have DRM, and Leaf can’t open them.

Does Leaf have a library or bookshelf?

No. Leaf opens books where they are, without importing or copying them, and remembers where you stopped reading.
